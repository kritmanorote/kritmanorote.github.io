Sky Dodge

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Sky Dodge (“the game”) handles your information.

What the game stores

The game has no accounts and no server of our own. Your progress — high score, coins, unlocked levels, shop purchases, missions, daily rewards and play stats — is stored on your device only. We never receive, share or have access to it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the game-over screen, an occasional full-screen ad, and rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a free revive. Google may collect and use the following to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you allow it in iOS’s “Allow tracking” prompt
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad impressions and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as performance and crash data

If you don’t allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they won’t be personalised. Players in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google’s consent form before any ads are served. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your rights and choices

  • Change tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • To answer the consent form again, delete and reinstall the game.
  • Delete everything the game stores by deleting the game.
  • Under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. For data held by Google, use Google’s tools linked above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
